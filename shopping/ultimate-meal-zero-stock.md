## Prototype 1 from this cart

Start with:

- 150–250 g dry rice, tuned to calorie target
- ~100 g dry black beans, batch-cooked
- ~250 g raw boneless chicken thighs
- 2 eggs
- ~400 g skyr
- 1 can sardines
- 60–100 g oats
- ~100 g frozen spinach
- ~100 g pepper
- ~200 g berries
- 15 g flax
- 20–30 g sunflower seeds
- 30–45 mL olive oil
- lime + iodized salt + garlic + cumin + chili

The original quantity set costs roughly $15.16 of food. Adding more rice/oats/oil/seeds to push the day beyond 3,000 kcal should keep the consumed-food cost around **$15–16** before optional upgrades.

## Buying rule

Every optional ingredient has to earn one of these jobs:

1. fixes a meaningful nutrient gap,
2. substantially improves taste or texture,
3. reduces active effort,
4. improves cost per useful calorie / protein / fibre,
5. increases willingness to repeat the meal.

Otherwise it stays on the shelf.
