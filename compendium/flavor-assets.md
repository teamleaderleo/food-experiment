# Reusable Flavor Assets

This file tracks the things that turn future cooking from **“start a recipe”** into **“pull one flavour asset + one protein/starch → dinner.”**

The project already has a condiment shelf. This is the next level: prepared sauces, marinades, cooked bases and freezer components that carry a meal’s identity.

---

## Asset class 1: bottled / jarred identity ingredients

These are worth buying because they remove the annoying work while preserving most of the flavour payoff.

### East / Southeast Asia

- **miso** → miso salmon, miso chicken, soup, glaze
- **gochujang** → Korean chicken, rice bowls, braises, sauce for meatballs
- **doubanjiang / chili-bean paste** → mapo tofu, spicy eggplant, noodles
- **Japanese curry roux** → curry rice, curry noodles, curry chicken
- **kecap manis** → ayam kecap, fried rice, sweet-soy glazes
- **laksa paste** → 10-minute noodle soup or full laksa
- **Thai curry paste** → chicken/shrimp/tofu curry
- **fish sauce** → Vietnamese, Thai, marinades, dipping sauces
- **chili crisp** → tofu, eggs, rice, dumplings, noodles

### Mexico / Latin

- **salsa roja** → bowls, chicken, eggs, tacos
- **salsa verde** → chicken batch, rice, quesadilla, enchiladas
- **chipotle in adobo** → chipotle-lime chicken, crema, beans, soup
- **prepared sofrito** → arroz con pollo, pollo guisado, rice/beans
- **aji amarillo paste** → Peruvian-ish chicken, creamy chile sauce
- **pickled jalapeños** → instant acid/heat/crunch
- **premade pico** → fresh texture without chopping
- **premade guacamole** → calorie-dense fresh finisher without avocado timing drama

### Middle East / Mediterranean

- **tahini** → shawarma bowls, tofu, roasted vegetables, yogurt/tahini sauce
- **toum** → chicken/rice/naan, sandwiches, potato
- **prepared chimichurri** → chicken, salmon, potatoes
- **tzatziki** → sumac chicken, pita, bowls
- **preserved lemon** → Moroccan/tagine lane
- **Ras el Hanout / shawarma blend / za’atar / sumac** → high identity per teaspoon

### Europe

- **pesto** → pasta, chicken, salmon, bread
- **good tomato sauce** → meatballs, pasta, baked dishes
- **Dijon** → salmon glaze, chicken, pan sauce

## Asset class 4: cooked sauce bases

### Cantonese master soy sauce

This is the flagship reusable cooked asset.

After braising chicken:

- strain the liquid
- cool promptly
- freeze
- reuse for a later batch

The Woks of Life explicitly describes reusing the reserved soy braising liquid multiple times.

Project: [`../experiments/002u-world-tour-round-2.md`](../experiments/002u-world-tour-round-2.md)

Source: [The Woks of Life — Soy Sauce Chicken](https://thewoksoflife.com/soy-sauce-chicken/)

### Curry base

A good curry sauce can be cooked in quantity and later combined with:

- chicken
- tofu
- shrimp
- potato / peas / spinach

Potentially useful for Thai curry, butter-chicken-ish sauce, Japanese curry and tomato/onion Indian curry lanes.

### Ragù / tomato-meat sauce

Cook once, then use over:

- pasta
- baked pasta
- polenta
- bread
- rice if that sounds good

### Mapo sauce base

Batch the seasoned meat/sauce portion; add tofu fresh or during reheat.

### Dan-dan sauce base

Premix sesame/tahini + soy + vinegar + chili components. Keep pork/ya-cai topping separate. Fresh noodles then become the only real cook step.

Project: [`../experiments/002l-batch-reheat-architecture.md`](../experiments/002l-batch-reheat-architecture.md)

## Asset class 6: freezer insurance

Freeze only when the food clearly benefits future-you.

Strong candidates:

- raw marinated chicken bags
- master soy sauce
- ragù
- curry
- adobo
- rou zao
- cooked salsa chicken
- baked-pasta portions
- chili / stew
- pesto/chimichurri in small portions if the product freezes well

Less attractive:

- delicate egg dishes
- fully dressed noodles
- fresh herbs
- foods that are already trivial to cook fresh

---

## A useful future freezer layout

The freezer does not need to become a warehouse. A small rotating set could be enough:

- 1–2 marinated chicken bags
- 1 fish option
- 1 cooked braise/curry portion
- 1 emergency frozen protein (meatballs/dumplings)
- 1 reusable sauce asset

That is enough to create several zero-planning dinners without inventory-management cosplay.

---

# Asset-building rule

Only save an asset if it passes both questions:

1. **Will this make a future meal substantially easier?**
2. **Will I actually be excited to eat what it makes?**

The goal is not a freezer full of clever projects. The goal is fewer decisions between hunger and good food.
