# Food Compendium

This directory is the cross-linked map of the whole project.

The experiments are where ideas are discovered. The compendium is where future-you should go when the question is:

> **What kind of food do I feel like, and what is the easiest good way to get there?**

## Start with the view that matches the decision

- [`recipes-by-cuisine.md`](recipes-by-cuisine.md) — pick a country / flavour family and see the strongest dishes, source recipes, and internal notes.
- [`recipes-by-workflow.md`](recipes-by-workflow.md) — pick the kind of effort available: air fryer, one-pot, braise, rice cooker, oven, batch, guest night, etc.
- [`recipe-matrix.md`](recipe-matrix.md) — compare many dishes at once by protein, starch, workflow, hand-fuss, leftovers, and guest payoff.
- [`meal-families.md`](meal-families.md) — see how dishes from different countries are variations on the same underlying cooking architecture: one-pot chicken/rice, braises, coconut curries, egg meals, fish in sauce, etc.
- [`rice-passport.md`](rice-passport.md) — browse meals where the rice/starch itself carries the cuisine identity: jollof, nasi lemak, lemon rice, tomato rice, pelau, bariis, kabsa, tahchin and more.
- [`identity-ingredients.md`](identity-ingredients.md) — pick one high-leverage jar, paste, spice, or prepared ingredient and see the cuisines and meals it unlocks.
- [`aromatic-bases.md`](aromatic-bases.md) — prepared / blended / frozen bases such as epis, sofrito, green seasoning, ginger-garlic paste and lemongrass paste that remove nightly chopping.
- [`flavor-assets.md`](flavor-assets.md) — sauces, pastes, marinades, cooked bases, and freezer assets that make later meals trivial.
- [`world-tour-index.md`](world-tour-index.md) — browse the expanding edible atlas geographically and jump into each world-tour research round.
- [`source-library.md`](source-library.md) — curated outbound links to strong original recipes and recipe sites worth revisiting.

For a short operating guide rather than a reference library, use [`../playbook/food-passport.md`](../playbook/food-passport.md).

## Core project idea

The project does not need one ideal meal.

It needs a **large menu with a small grocery vocabulary**.

The same reusable ingredients should travel through many cuisines:

- chicken thighs / wings
- salmon / mackerel
- tofu
- eggs
- skyr / yogurt
- rice
- pasta / noodles
- tortillas / naan / bread
- frozen vegetables
- prepared aromatics
- a deliberately chosen shelf of sauces and pastes

Variety comes mostly from the flavour system and cooking format rather than buying a completely new grocery list every night.

## Current rules of the road

### Cooking

Prefer phone-compatible cooking:

- Ziploc marinade
- air fryer
- rice cooker
- oven / sheet pan
- simmer / braise
- one pot
- slow cooker / pressure cooker
- reheating genuinely good leftovers

Elapsed time is cheap when hands and attention are free.

### Protein

Everyday default:

> **chicken + fish + tofu + eggs + dairy first; pork/beef when their flavour payoff is worth it.**

### Chopping

Buy away chopping when it is the exact thing preventing the meal:

- premade pico
- prepared sofrito
- frozen diced onion / pepper
- garlic / ginger paste
- jarred salsa
- prepared chimichurri
- pickled onions

Or do the chopping once, freeze the result, and stop repeating it. See [`aromatic-bases.md`](aromatic-bases.md).

### Batch philosophy

Cook a good dinner in a slightly excessive quantity, eat it, refrigerate the next portion, and freeze obvious surplus.

See [`../experiments/002l-batch-reheat-architecture.md`](../experiments/002l-batch-reheat-architecture.md).

## Status vocabulary

The compendium uses a few loose labels:

- **Control-level** — competes with rice cooker + air fryer in ordinary life.
- **Phone-compatible** — longer elapsed time is fine because attention density is low.
- **Normal cooking pays** — more hands-on work, but taste / leftovers / guest payoff justify it.
- **Guest mode** — disproportionately impressive relative to technique.
- **Curiosity** — worth trying because it opens a genuinely new world, even if it is not weekly food.

Nothing becomes a permanent recipe merely because it sounds good. Promotion still requires repeated real-life use.
