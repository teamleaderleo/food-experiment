# Recipes by Cuisine / Flavour Family

Use this when the question is **“Where do I want dinner to take me?”**

Each section points to project notes and, where useful, a strong external source recipe.

## Taiwan / Chinese comfort

### Rou zao fan

Sweet-savory ground-pork braise over rice. Excellent batch food; use pork as a richer rotation protein rather than an everyday default.

- Project: [`../experiments/002i-flagship-prototypes.md`](../experiments/002i-flagship-prototypes.md)
- Workflow: braise + rice cooker
- Best use: normal cooking that pays / leftovers

### Mapo tofu

Keep tofu dominant; use 75–125 g pork when the pork flavour is worth it, or ground chicken for an everyday version.

- Project: [`../experiments/002q-white-meat-fish-tofu-first.md`](../experiments/002q-white-meat-fish-tofu-first.md)
- Source: [Just One Cookbook — Easy Mapo Tofu](https://www.justonecookbook.com/mapo-tofu/)
- Workflow: one pan + rice

### Dan dan noodles

Batch the pork/ya-cai topping and sauce base; cook noodles fresh when possible.

- Project: [`../experiments/002i-flagship-prototypes.md`](../experiments/002i-flagship-prototypes.md)
- Workflow: batch topping + fresh noodles

### Three-cup chicken

Chicken, soy, sesame oil, rice wine, garlic, ginger and basil. Strong Taiwanese chicken lane without leaning on red meat.

- Project: [`../experiments/002j-more-comfort-food-branches.md`](../experiments/002j-more-comfort-food-branches.md)
- Workflow: braise / reduction

### Cantonese soy-sauce chicken + master sauce

The recipe is useful; the bigger idea is saving and freezing the braising liquid as a future flavour asset.

- Project: [`../experiments/002u-world-tour-round-2.md`](../experiments/002u-world-tour-round-2.md)
- Source: [The Woks of Life — Soy Sauce Chicken](https://thewoksoflife.com/soy-sauce-chicken/)
- Workflow: braise / pressure cooker
- Asset: reusable master sauce

## Japan

### Oyakodon

### Soboro don

- Project: [`../experiments/002q-white-meat-fish-tofu-first.md`](../experiments/002q-white-meat-fish-tofu-first.md)

Cold tofu + soy/ponzu + chili crisp/furikake/bonito. Savory analogue of the direct-from-tub skyr habit.

# What this index is telling us

The project now has several independent sources of variety:

1. **country / flavour family**
2. **protein**
3. **starch**
4. **cooking format**
5. **fresh finishers**
6. **effort level**

That means repetition at the grocery level does not have to feel repetitive at the dinner level.
