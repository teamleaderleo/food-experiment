# Meal Families — Cross-Cuisine Map

Use this when the question is less **“which country?”** and more **“what kind of cooking do I feel like?”**

The point is to recognize that many great dishes are variations on a small number of recurring meal architectures.

That makes unfamiliar food easier to understand and makes the grocery overlap obvious.

# 1. One-pot chicken + rice

## Core architecture

> chicken + aromatic base + rice + broth + one flavour identity → cover and cook

This is one of the highest-value families in the whole project because the protein and starch finish together.

### Members

- **Rice-cooker Hainanese chicken rice** — ginger/garlic/scallion/dashi-ish lane  
  [`002N`](../experiments/002n-control-level-convenience-branches.md)
- **Puerto Rican arroz con pollo** — sofrito/tomato/olive lane  
  [`002U`](../experiments/002u-world-tour-round-2.md)
- **Brazilian galinhada** — turmeric/tomato/pepper/peas lane  
  [`002V`](../experiments/002v-world-tour-round-3.md)
- **Chinese-style one-pot chicken rice** — soy/ginger/vegetable lane  
  [`002Q`](../experiments/002q-white-meat-fish-tofu-first.md)
- **Mexican one-pot chicken rice** — tomato/chile/corn lane  
  [`002Q`](../experiments/002q-white-meat-fish-tofu-first.md)
- **Spanish chicken rice** — smoked paprika/tomato/olive lane  
  candidate for future testing

## Grocery lesson

The base barely changes:

- chicken thighs
- rice
- frozen onion/pepper
- garlic paste
- stock

The destination comes from sofrito / soy / paprika / turmeric / chile / olives / herbs.

# 4. Coconut curry / coconut stew

## Core architecture

> curry paste/spice base + coconut milk + protein + vegetable/starch + acid/salt

This is one of the easiest ways to create huge cuisine shifts with shelf-stable ingredients.

### Members

  [`002S`](../experiments/002s-guest-worthy-low-fuss.md)
- Thai massaman  
  [`002V`](../experiments/002v-world-tour-round-3.md)
- Sri Lankan chicken curry  
  [`002V`](../experiments/002v-world-tour-round-3.md)
- Kerala fish curry  
  [`002V`](../experiments/002v-world-tour-round-3.md)
- Brazilian moqueca  
  [`002V`](../experiments/002v-world-tour-round-3.md)
- tofu-spinach-pea curry  
  [`002Q`](../experiments/002q-white-meat-fish-tofu-first.md)

## Variations come from

- curry paste / spice blend
- fish sauce vs tamarind vs lime
- tomato vs no tomato
- fish vs chicken vs tofu
- potato vs rice vs noodles
- curry leaves / lemongrass / pandan / herbs

## Bulk note

Coconut milk is calorie-dense, so these meals naturally fit the bulk. Rotate rather than making coconut milk the only fat source every day.

  [`002V`](../experiments/002v-world-tour-round-3.md)

## Why this deserves its own family

The broth is easy to eat quickly, while noodles and coconut provide serious calories.

- boiled egg
- fried shallots
- chili crisp/oil

# The useful meta-pattern

Most of the world’s food does not require learning a totally new cooking method.

Usually one of these happens:

1. the **same method** gets a new spice / sauce identity;
2. the **same protein + starch** are arranged in a new format;
3. one new condiment unlocks several dishes at once;
4. a dish that looks unfamiliar is actually a familiar family in different clothes.

That is why the project can keep expanding without the kitchen becoming complicated.
