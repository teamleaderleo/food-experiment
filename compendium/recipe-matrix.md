# Recipe Matrix

This is the bird’s-eye view.

Use it when choosing between meals rather than browsing one cuisine at a time.

Legend:

- **Fuss:** very low / low / medium / high
- **Phone:** how compatible the cooking is with attention going elsewhere
- **Leftovers:** weak / okay / strong
- **Guest:** low / medium / high

All ratings are working judgments, not laboratory measurements. Real-life cooking can overrule them.

# Matrix principle

The ideal long-run menu probably has representatives in several cells rather than one winner:

- 5 ultra-low-fuss meals
- 5 bag-marinated chicken/fish identities
- 5 braise/batch dishes
- 3–5 meatless meals
- 3–5 guest dishes
- 3 emergency freezer meals

That is already enough to make ordinary eating feel enormous.
