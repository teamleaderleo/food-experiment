# Rice Passport — When the Starch Carries the Cuisine

Use this when the question is:

> **What can I do to rice so the meal feels like a completely different destination before I even choose the protein?**

This compendium exists because the project originally treated rice mostly as a neutral calorie carrier. That leaves a huge amount of culinary variety unused.

A strong alternative architecture is:

> **flavoured rice + very simple protein + one condiment / vegetable = full cuisine identity**

That can be even easier than making a complicated protein dish.

## Tahdig skill tree

Crispy Persian rice crust deserves to be learned as a technique independent of any single recipe.

Potential future lanes:

- saffron tahdig

# Project implication

The project now has two equally strong ways to create variety:

## Protein-first passport

> plain rice + globally flavoured chicken/fish/tofu

Examples:

- Vietnamese lemongrass chicken
- jerk chicken
- shawarma
- miso salmon
- gochujang chicken

## Starch-first passport

> globally flavoured rice + simple protein

Examples:

- jollof + roast chicken
- lemon rice + egg
- nasi lemak rice + chicken
- tomato rice + yogurt
- bariis / kabsa / pelau

The second model may reduce nightly marinade/sauce work even further.

## Current thesis

Rice is not merely fuel.

It can be the lowest-effort place to hide a whole country's flavour system.
