# Recipes that earned their place

This directory is intentionally sparse.

A meal belongs here after repeated real-life use. Promotion means:

- it tastes good enough to crave again
- the active effort feels reasonable on an ordinary evening
- the groceries get used naturally
- cleanup feels acceptable
- the meal has survived at least a few repetitions

When promoting an experiment, record the version actually used most often. Keep optional flourishes optional.
