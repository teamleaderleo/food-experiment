# Food Passport — Quick-Start Playbook

**Status:** working operating manual

The project has grown past one “ultimate meal.” The better idea is a small personal food universe:

> **buy a boring, reusable grocery backbone; pick a flavour destination; use the lowest-fuss cooking mode that still gives a great result; make extra when leftovers are good.**

The goal is that future-you can open this file, buy a few things, follow one linked experiment, and eat something excellent without re-deriving the whole project.

---

## 1. The thing every new meal has to respect

The current real-world benchmark is extremely strong:

- rice cooker white rice
- air-fryer chicken wings / chicken
- arugula
- olive oil
- freshly grated garlic
- skyr with seeds mixed directly into the tub

See: [`defaults/current-default.md`](../defaults/current-default.md)

New food can win through taste, variety, nutrition, eating ease, batchability, or guest-worthiness. It does not need to be faster than the benchmark if the extra effort has a clear payoff.

---

## 2. Cooking style: phone-compatible first

Elapsed time is cheap when the food mostly handles itself.

Prefer:

- rice cooker
- air fryer
- oven / sheet pan
- simmering / braising
- one-pot meals
- Ziploc marinades
- slow cooker / pressure cooker
- batch bakes
- reheating good leftovers

Avoid turning normal dinner into handcraft unless the result is genuinely special:

- breading stations
- shaping meatballs/dumplings by hand
- multi-bowl mixing
- constant wok babysitting
- lots of fine chopping
- delicate multi-timer techniques

See: [`experiments/002p-phone-compatible-cooking.md`](../experiments/002p-phone-compatible-cooking.md)

## 3. Protein default

The long-run default is:

> **chicken + fish + tofu + eggs + dairy first; pork/beef when their flavour is worth it.**

This keeps the everyday rotation aligned with digestive preference while preserving excellent pork/beef dishes as real treats.

### Everyday backbone

- chicken thighs
- wings
- ground chicken / minced thigh
- salmon / mackerel
- tofu
- eggs
- skyr / yogurt / milk

### Rotation / cameo proteins

- ground pork for mapo, dan dan, rou zao fan
- thin beef for gyudon / hayashi
- shrimp for quick seafood meals

See: [`experiments/002q-white-meat-fish-tofu-first.md`](../experiments/002q-white-meat-fish-tofu-first.md)

## 6. Strong first-pass recipes

If choosing without thinking, these are unusually high-payoff:

1. **Vietnamese lemongrass chicken** — bag marinade, air fryer, rice.
2. **Miso salmon** — bag marinade, air fryer/broiler, rice.
3. **Chipotle-lime chicken** — bag marinade, air fryer, premade pico, skyr crema.
4. **One-pan baked butter chicken** — about 10 minutes setup, oven does the rest.
5. **Portuguese peri-peri chicken + potatoes** — one pan, drippings season the potatoes.
6. **Oyakodon** — soft chicken/egg/rice, one pan, very easy eating.
7. **Chicken adobo** — simple braise, excellent leftovers.
8. **Rou zao fan** — ground-pork braise, batch-friendly, use as a richer rotation meal.
9. **Shawarma chicken rice** — Ziploc marinade + air fryer + toum/tahini/pickles.
10. **Whole side of salmon** — extremely high guest-wow per unit effort.

## 7. Light meal prep, not meal-prep cosplay

Default rhythm:

> **cook tonight → fridge tomorrow’s portion → maybe fridge one more → freeze obvious surplus.**

Batch the parts that reheat well. Keep fresh finishers separate only when quality benefits.

High-value fresh finishing moves:

- lemon/lime squeeze
- premade pico
- pickles
- toum / tahini
- chili crisp
- grated parmesan
- fried shallots
- fresh herbs when available

See: [`experiments/002l-batch-reheat-architecture.md`](../experiments/002l-batch-reheat-architecture.md)

## 8. Guest mode

When someone else is eating, do not suddenly become a pastry chef.

Use the same workflow with a visually strong centerpiece:

- whole side of salmon
- Chicken Marbella
- chicken tagine
- Moroccan chicken
- Thai red curry
- baked pasta

Then add one easy starch and one bright finisher.

## 9. The vacation principle

The grocery cart can stay repetitive while dinner changes countries.

The same chicken thighs can become:

- Vietnamese lemongrass
- Portuguese peri-peri
- Korean gochujang braise
- Greek lemon-garlic
- Indian butter chicken
- Moroccan lemon/olive
- Mexican chipotle
- shawarma
- Filipino adobo

That is the core trick:

> **boring inventory, exciting dinner.**

A meal does not need to be perfect. It needs to be tasty enough that making it again sounds good.
