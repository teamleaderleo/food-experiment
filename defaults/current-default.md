# Current default meal pattern

This is the pre-experiment baseline. It exists so future changes can be compared against something real.

## Main meal

- chicken wings
  - frozen/pre-made when convenience wins
  - home-marinated when there is a little more time
  - usually cooked in the air fryer
- white rice
- arugula
- olive oil

## Skyr bowl

- Icelandic skyr
- flaxseed powder
- hemp hearts
- sunflower seeds
- assorted nuts/seeds as available
- cacao nibs sometimes

## Why this default works

- very little active cooking
- easy air-fryer workflow
- predictable groceries
- easy protein anchor from chicken and skyr
- arugula requires essentially zero preparation
- rice is cheap, neutral, and easy

## Friction points worth testing

- arugula can be relatively expensive compared with frozen vegetables
- white rice contributes little fiber and relatively little protein
- frozen vegetables are cheaper and keep longer, though they require heating
- takeout becomes attractive when the home option feels repetitive

## Current direction

Keep the convenient parts of this baseline while testing beans, tortillas, frozen vegetables, and flexible sauces. Rice can remain in rotation and share the plate with beans.
