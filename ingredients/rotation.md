# Ingredient rotation

A list of groceries that combine easily with each other. Keep this biased toward ingredients that survive long enough to get used.

## Protein anchors

### Chicken wings

**Role:** existing default; easy air-fryer protein.  
**Pairs with:** rice, beans, frozen vegetables, tortillas, salsa.

### Icelandic skyr

**Role:** easy protein-heavy snack/bowl; can also become a creamy savory sauce.  
**Pairs with:** flaxseed powder, hemp hearts, sunflower seeds, nuts, cacao nibs, lime, garlic, chile sauces.

### Black beans

**Role:** pantry-friendly protein + fiber; can share or replace part of the rice portion.  
**Pairs with:** rice, tortillas, chicken, corn, peppers, salsa, lime, skyr.

### Pinto beans

**Role:** next bean candidate; especially promising mashed or refried-style.  
**Status:** future experiment.

## Carbohydrate bases

### White rice

**Role:** familiar, cheap, neutral, easy.  
**Current idea:** use alongside beans instead of treating rice as the entire base.

### Tortillas

**Role:** turn components and leftovers into tacos/wraps with almost no assembly work.  
**Experiment:** compare corn and flour in actual weeknight use.

## Vegetables

### Arugula

**Best feature:** open bag and eat. Excellent zero-cooking vegetable.  
**Tradeoff:** price and short fridge life.

### Frozen peppers + onions

**Best feature:** taco/bowl compatible, long freezer life, no chopping.  
**Test:** whether the heating step feels easy enough in practice.

### Frozen corn

**Best feature:** cheap, sweet, easy with beans/rice/tacos.

### Frozen broccoli / green beans / spinach / mixed vegetables

**Best feature:** inexpensive long-lived options for meals outside the taco lane.

### Cabbage

**Best feature:** cheap, crunchy, long fridge life, useful raw in tacos.  
**Status:** future test.

## Flavor multipliers

Keep several of these around so the same base ingredients can taste different across the week:

- salsa
- limes
- cumin
- garlic powder
- chile powder / chile seasoning
- olive oil
- hot sauce
- pickled onions, future test
- chipotle-style sauce, future test

## Skyr bowl add-ins

Current rotation:

- flaxseed powder
- hemp hearts
- sunflower seeds
- assorted nuts/seeds
- cacao nibs sometimes

## Buying principle

Prefer groceries that can participate in several meals. A strong candidate should combine with at least three other regular ingredients and survive long enough to be used without planning the whole week around it.
