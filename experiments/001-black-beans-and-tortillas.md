# Experiment 001: Black beans + tortillas

**Date started:** 2026-09-07  
**Status:** planned

## Question

Can black beans and tortillas become a cheap, satisfying, low-attention alternative to leaning so heavily on white rice, while still fitting the existing chicken/air-fryer routine?

## First-pass rule

Use **canned black beans**. This experiment is about whether beans naturally fit the week, so convenience comes first. Dried beans can get their own experiment later.

## Core groceries

- 2–4 cans black beans
- corn tortillas
- flour tortillas, optional for comparison
- frozen pepper-and-onion mix
- frozen corn
- salsa
- limes
- plain skyr
- cumin
- garlic powder
- chili powder or another preferred chile seasoning
- salt
- existing chicken wings or another easy chicken option
- rice already in the pantry, if available

## Five-minute bean method

1. Drain and rinse one can of black beans.
2. Put them in a small pot or microwave-safe bowl.
3. Add cumin, garlic powder, chile seasoning, salt, and a splash of water.
4. Heat until hot.
5. Taste and adjust salt/acidity. Lime can go in at the end.

Optional move: mash roughly one-third of the beans. This makes them creamier and helps them stay inside tortillas.

## Easy lime-skyr sauce

Mix plain skyr with lime juice and salt. Garlic powder can join if it improves things. Keep the ratio informal and adjust by taste.

## Iteration A — simplest possible tacos

- black beans
- warmed tortillas
- salsa
- lime-skyr sauce

Purpose: find out whether the bean/tortilla combination is enjoyable on its own before chicken starts carrying the meal.

## Iteration B — chicken + bean tacos

- air-fryer chicken
- black beans
- tortillas
- frozen peppers/onions
- salsa
- lime-skyr sauce

Purpose: test whether beans slot naturally into the current chicken routine.

## Iteration C — half rice / half beans bowl

- white rice
- black beans
- chicken
- frozen corn and/or peppers
- salsa
- skyr sauce or olive oil

Purpose: keep the familiar rice meal while increasing the legume share. Compare satiety and enjoyment with the old rice-heavy version.

## Iteration D — mashed bean tortilla

- roughly mashed seasoned beans
- tortilla
- salsa
- optional chicken or frozen vegetables

Purpose: test the lowest-mess form. If it holds together well, this could become a very easy lunch or late dinner.

## Iteration E — tortilla comparison

Make the same filling once with corn tortillas and once with flour tortillas.

Record:

- which tastes better
- which reheats better
- which tears less
- which feels more filling
- which package size gets eaten before going stale
