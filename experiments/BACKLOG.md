Test which frozen vegetables give the best combination of price, convenience, taste, and willingness to actually eat them.

Candidates:

- broccoli
- green beans
- spinach
- peppers + onions

## Rule for adding experiments

Prefer an experiment that answers one useful question with groceries that overlap the existing rotation. Avoid buying a dozen special ingredients simply to perform the test.
